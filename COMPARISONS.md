# Statly compared with other short-form video tools

Most social media tools rank accounts, count followers or build client reports. **Statly answers a narrower question: did this video actually work for the account that posted it, and is it repeatable?** It scores every video against its own account's median views, tracks public Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and X accounts daily, and needs no social network login.

Full, up-to-date write-ups live on the Statly website: [all comparisons](https://trystatly.com/alternatives). Competitor prices and features below are summarised from those pages (2026) and can change: check each tool's own site before deciding.

## When another tool fits better

- **You need analytics for your own accounts' private data** (reach, demographics, DMs): Statly reads public data only, by design.
- **You need Facebook, Snapchat, LinkedIn or Pinterest:** Statly covers Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and X.
- **You need refreshes more often than daily:** Statly updates once a day.
- **You want AI to write the scripts for you:** Statly gives you the data and leaves the creative decisions to you.

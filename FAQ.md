# Frequently Asked Questions

Have a question that isn't answered here? Email [info.statly@gmail.com](mailto:info.statly@gmail.com).

### What exactly does Statly do?

Statly is a Chrome extension that overlays a set of analytics dashboards on **Instagram and TikTok**. Open it on any profile to audit that account's Reels or videos, compare creators, merge multiple accounts, track a watchlist, and find trends — then export everything to a spreadsheet, all without leaving the page.

### Does Statly work on TikTok too, or just Instagram?

Both. TikTok analytics now has full feature parity with Instagram: Audit, Compare, Merge, Watchlist, Trend Finder, KPIs, and Charts all work the same way on TikTok as they do on Instagram.

### Does Statly work on any profile?

Yes. It works on any **public** Instagram or TikTok profile, including your own and your competitors'. You stay signed into the platform as normal — Statly reads the public engagement data it already shows.

### Do I need to connect my Meta or TikTok account?

No. There's no OAuth flow, no API key from Meta or TikTok, and no permissions requested beyond what's needed to read the public page you're already viewing.

### Can I scan a profile I'm not currently viewing?

Yes, on both platforms. Type any @username into Audit (or Compare/Merge/Watchlist) and Statly scans it in the background without you needing to navigate there yourself.

### Are more platforms coming?

Yes — Twitter/X, Reddit, and YouTube are listed as "Soon" in the extension's platform picker. See [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) for the current status.

### What's the difference between the dashboards?

- **Audit** — the deepest look at a single profile.
- **Compare** — puts two profiles side by side.
- **Merge** — combines several accounts from the same creator into one report.
- **Watchlist** — saves profiles so you can scan them all at once.
- **Trend Finder** — pulls the latest Reels from your saved profiles and surfaces what's trending.

### What data gets exported?

A structured spreadsheet with the profile, post URL, creation date and time, views, likes, comments, captions, and duration — one row per Reel/video, sorted however you scanned. TikTok exports also include shares and saves. Export it directly or copy it straight into Google Sheets in the same format.

### What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free gives you 3 scans per day on the last 30 Reels, with the full Audit dashboard, library, and exports. Pro unlocks unlimited scans, deeper ranges (60/90/180/custom), and the Compare, Merge, Watchlist, and Trend Finder dashboards, plus API access.

### Will Statly slow down my browser?

No. Statly only activates when you open it, and it's built to stay light — it sits quietly until you ask it to scan.

### Can I use Statly for free?

Yes. Install it from the Chrome Web Store and start scanning right away — no account or sign-up needed for the free tier.

### How far back can a scan go?

Up to 300 Reels or 180 days per profile, depending on your plan and the range you choose.

### Can I download the actual Reel/video files?

Yes — any scanned Reel or TikTok video can be downloaded as a clean MP4, with no watermark, directly from the results view.

### Is there an API for developers?

Yes. The Statly API lets your own software pull a profile's full report — including basic and executive KPIs — as structured JSON, instead of going through the extension UI. API access is included with Pro plans.

### Where is my data stored?

Statly runs the scan against the public page you're viewing in your browser. See the [Privacy Policy](https://trystatly.com/privacy.html) for full details on what is and isn't stored.

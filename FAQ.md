## About Statly

### What is Statly?

Statly is a short-form video research tool. It tracks public Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and X accounts every day and shows which videos are beating their own account's normal results (outliers), which are growing fastest right now, and what is trending across your niche. It has two parts: the **Statly web app** ([app.trystatly.com](https://app.trystatly.com)), which tracks accounts daily, and the free **Statly Chrome extension**, for quick audits while you browse.

### Who is Statly for?

Content creators, social media managers, marketing teams, agencies running several clients, and brands researching competitors or creators.

### Which platforms does Statly support?

Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and X (Twitter), in both the web app and the Chrome extension. The extension also audits long YouTube videos.

### What is an outlier video?

A video whose views are far above the usual views of the account that posted it. Statly's **outlier score** is a video's views divided by its account's median views: about 2× is worth studying, 5× is strong, 10× is exceptional. Full guide: [What is an outlier video?](https://trystatly.com/what-is-an-outlier-video)

### Why compare a video with its own account instead of looking at total views?

Because big accounts get big views on a normal day. Comparing each video with its own account's median shows what actually worked, so a small creator's breakout is as visible as a large account's.

### Do I need to connect my Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or X account?

No. Statly reads public data only and never asks for a social network password. You can track any public account, including competitors you don't own.

### Can Statly track private accounts?

No. Private accounts can't be tracked; Statly says so on the account.

## The web app

### How often does the data update?

Once a day. Every video and post inside your scan window is re-read at the hour you choose, on Statly's servers, so the data is ready even when your computer is off. Statly is not real-time.

### How far back does Statly look?

You choose a scan window of the last 7, 14 or 30 days. Every video posted inside it is tracked and refreshed daily.

### What do I see for each video?

Views, likes, comments, shares and saves where the platform shows them, engagement rate, like and comment rate, views compared with the account's average and median, the outlier score, and the video's growth day by day.

### What is the Trend Finder?

A view across all your niches of the videos growing fastest right now (compared with each account's normal day), this week's top outliers and top sounds, with filters and sorting.

### What is the Watchlist?

Your tracked accounts grouped into niches, with each account's full table of videos, its baseline, growth, posting times and video lengths.

### Can my team use it?

Yes. Workspaces have viewer, editor and admin roles. Starter includes 5 seats, Scale 10, Agency and Enterprise unlimited. Agencies can keep one workspace per client.

### Can I get alerts?

Yes. Statly can post alerts in Slack, Discord or Telegram when a video takes off.

### Can I download videos?

Yes. Save videos to your Library and download them for reference. On X, you can save a post's pictures and text.

### Can I export the data?

Yes. The Watchlist, the Trend Finder and each account's videos export to CSV, and Scale and above include the API.

## Plans and pricing

### How much does Statly cost?

Starter €39 a month (€390 a year), Scale €89 a month (€890 a year), Agency €179 a month (€1,790 a year), and custom Enterprise plans. Current prices: [trystatly.com/pricing](https://trystatly.com/pricing).

### Is there a free trial?

Yes, 7 days. The trial asks for a card at checkout and becomes your plan after 7 days unless you cancel. During the trial you can track up to 20 accounts and 500 videos.

### What does "videos tracked at once" mean?

Every video inside your scan window, across all the accounts you track. Starter holds 2,000, Scale 5,000, Agency 10,000.

### What happens if I go over my plan?

Nothing stops. Statly keeps refreshing each account's newest videos first, and the oldest ones keep their last numbers until there is room. Scale and Agency can add extra videos: +1,000 for €20 a month, up to +2,000.

### Which plans include the API and MCP?

Scale, Agency and Enterprise, from the first paid day. Not Starter, and not during the free trial.

## The Chrome extension

### Is the Chrome extension free?

Yes, and it needs no Statly account. The free limits are 3 scans a day (shared by Audit, Compare and Merge), and a Watchlist and Trend Finder of 2 niches with 3 accounts each. Every web app plan, the 7-day free trial included, removes the limits once you connect the extension to your account.

### What is the difference between the extension and the web app?

The extension scans an account when you click, inside your browser, for a quick look. The web app tracks your accounts every day on Statly's servers, keeps their history and growth, and adds teams, alerts, the Library, the API and MCP.

### How do I connect the extension to the web app?

In the web app, open Settings → Chrome extension and connect. A connected browser gets the extension's full scans while your plan or free trial is running. Teammates with the Viewer role stay on the free limits, because a Viewer can only read.

## API and MCP

### What is the Statly API?

A read-only REST API for your workspace: niches, tracked accounts, videos and posts with outlier scores, top sounds and the Library. Keys are created in the app under Settings → API & MCP. Limits: 60 calls a minute and 10,000 a day per workspace. Docs: [api.trystatly.com](https://api.trystatly.com).

### What is the Statly MCP?

An MCP server at `https://mcp.trystatly.com/mcp` that lets Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor read one Statly workspace (including post thumbnails) and answer questions about it in plain English. It can't change anything.

## Data and privacy

### What data does Statly collect about the accounts I track?

Only public information: the videos and posts, their public numbers, and the account's public profile details. See the [Privacy Policy](https://trystatly.com/privacy).

### Is Statly affiliated with Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or X?

No. Statly is an independent tool and is not endorsed by Meta, TikTok, Google or X.
